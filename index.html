NOTES / RESEARCH / CONTEXT

Inspect the manual, too.

RE:PAIR is a creative guide by Krystal Joy Brown, made for Critical Experiences at NYU IMA. It explores attention, cultural hegemony, conscious choice, and kinship through the visual language of an assembly manual. The “repair” is a practice of agency; it is not a promise that a small exercise can resolve a structural problem.

Research behind the reveals +
  1. Meta / Instagram Feed Ranking System Card. Platform explanation of how interactions and preferences inform feed ranking. This does not guarantee that engaging with a post will increase its reach.
  2. Epstein, Lin, Pennycook & Rand (2022), Quantifying attention via dwell time and engagement in a social media browsing environment. Experimental research distinguishes viewing attention from the decision to engage. This is not a diagnosis of an individual user.
  3. Gallup / The Importance of Employee Recognition: Low Cost, High Impact, updated January 12, 2024. Reports that one in three U.S. workers strongly agreed they received recognition or praise for good work in the prior seven days. This is not equivalent to a measure of feeling undervalued.
  4. Interview with Kristen McGuiness, October 5, 2026. Excerpts checked against the supplied Zoom transcript: 12:10:01, 12:14:14, and 12:28:46. Her account of storytelling and community informs the guide’s approach.
  5. Interview with Alexander Williams Tolbert. Excerpts checked against the supplied Zoom transcript: 16:12:35, 16:13:06, and 16:17:20. His discussion of socially formed beliefs, standards of success, and revision informs the guide’s critical lens.

Interview excerpts represent the speakers’ perspectives. Purchasing, message inspection, and kinship prompts are reflective exercises written for this guide. The branching background is a visual metaphor for thought and connection, not an anatomical diagram.

How the exposure illustration works +

Illustrated encounters = minutes × assumed posts per minute. The default is 6 posts per minute, chosen to demonstrate the arithmetic—not as a research finding. Adjust the rate from 1 to 20. Encounters can include repeated posts and are not measured advertising impressions, unique messages, or proof of influence.

Print / fold / carry +

Use your browser’s print dialog to save or print a reading copy. Every notice, reveal, and repair is included. An accordion-fold physical edition is a future design iteration.
